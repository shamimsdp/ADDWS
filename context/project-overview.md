# Project overview

## Purpose

Help clothing brands manage every style, color, size, outlet, warehouse and online channel from one reliable inventory system, then improve buying and replenishment decisions using trustworthy data.

## Confirmed product shape

Multi-tenant SaaS with store retail and automated online integrations in the first release. Bangladesh-first localization follows the supplied research; international expansion is a future option.

## Proposed initial customer

Growing clothing brands with multiple outlets, a warehouse and online sales. Validate with three to five design partners before fixing scale assumptions.

## Users

Owner/admin, store manager, cashier, inventory/warehouse operator, purchaser, online order agent and accountant. Access is scoped by tenant, role and location; cost visibility is a separate permission.

## First-release capabilities

Apparel catalog and barcode labels; inventory ledger and balances; suppliers, purchase orders and partial receipts; transfers; counts and adjustments; POS and cash shifts; returns/exchanges; customers; basic discounts; automated ecommerce orders/stock sync; courier booking/tracking and COD reconciliation; reports; imports; localization; audit; tenant administration and recovery.

Begin with a bounded connector set. Provider selection remains open. Offline POS is a proposed requirement requiring an explicit stock-conflict policy.

## Deferred scope

Full accounting ERP, payroll, manufacturing/CMT, franchise settlement, advanced WMS, multiple native mobile apps, unrestricted promotion combinations, forecasting optimization and autonomous AI purchasing/pricing.

## Success measures to establish with pilots

Stock variance, oversell incidents, checkout time, synchronization lag, courier/COD reconciliation exceptions, exchange completion time, import error rate and support time per tenant. Record a baseline and agree targets before promising improvements.
