# UI rules

- Optimize for daily work: scan, receive, transfer, count, sell and exchange.
- Make tenant and active location visible. Warn before a location change invalidates work.
- Present apparel data as style/color/size matrices with ordered sizes and explicit SKU details.
- Label physical, reserved, available, inbound and in-transit quantities distinctly.
- Display last sync time and offline/queued/conflicted status beside relevant POS and stock views.
- Do not imply stale stock is live or an unconfirmed provider payment is settled.
- Use visible price/tax/discount breakdowns and exchange difference settlement.
- Require reasons and appropriate authorization for stock adjustments, exceptional discounts and refunds.
- Show import preview, invalid rows and posting consequences before committing opening stock.
- Provide keyboard/scanner navigation, visible focus, accessible labels and touch-friendly POS targets.
- Never rely on color alone for status. Provide text and icons where helpful.
- Design loading, empty, error, offline, retry and permission-denied states explicitly.
- Support English/Bangla layout and BDT formatting without hard-coding text inside components.
- Protect cost and financial views according to permissions; hidden buttons do not enforce security.
- Keep operational screens focused on business outcomes. Integration technical detail belongs in admin diagnostics.
- Keep destructive or irreversible business actions explicit; routine navigation and reversible edits should stay quick.
