# Roadmap

Status: proposed sequence, not dated delivery commitments. Staffing and budget are unknown.

| Stage | Deliverables | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Pilot workflows, initial providers, hardware, offline policy, valuation, tax review, scale and success measures | Reviewed first-release scope and business rules |
| Foundation | Tenancy/access, catalog matrix, imports, locations, stock ledger, audit, basic UI | Tenant boundary and ledger invariants demonstrated |
| Retail operations | PO/receipts, transfers/counts, POS/shifts, returns/exchanges, basic reports | Complete warehouse-to-sale-to-exchange workflow reconciles |
| Automated online operations | Ecommerce stock/order sync, reservations, fulfillment, one courier, tracking, COD and returned goods | Duplicate/out-of-order events and failure recovery verified |
| First-release pilot | Offline behavior, migration, hardware, backups/restores, onboarding, tenant administration | Store and automated online operations run together with reconciled stock |
| Growth | Additional connectors, loyalty, mobile warehouse workflows, replenishment/allocation, analytics expansion | Measured operational value with supportable costs |
| Intelligence | Backtested forecasting, size curves, transfer recommendations, governed analytics assistant | Evidence of useful forecasts and traceable recommendations |
| Enterprise | Advanced WMS, SSO, multiple legal entities, SLA/private tenancy, optional production/CMT | Validated customer demand and operational readiness |

Retail and online delivery stages are parts of the same first release. Neither stage alone completes the confirmed MVP.

Advanced markdown optimization, external trend feeds and controlled automation follow validated data quality and measured benefit.
