## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Qabasic — practical Bangla ebooks you can own: buy once, read online on any device or download the PDF.
**What it does:** Digital bookstore and independent publisher (qabasic.com). Sells practical, how-to ebooks written in Bangla — cooking (everyday, budget and health-specific) and AI/automation — as personalised PDFs delivered to an online library with a built-in page-turning reader.
**Product category:** Bangla ebooks / practical Bangla guides (how customers search: "বাংলা রেসিপি বই", "রান্নার বই PDF", "ডায়াবেটিস রেসিপি", "ChatGPT বাংলা বই", "AI শেখার বই").
**Product type:** E-commerce, digital products (info products).
**Business model:** One-time purchase per book, prices ৳149–৳299; collections (AI Practical Series ৳699 for 4). Extra copies become gift codes. Payment by bKash Send Money + TrxID, confirmed by hand.

## Target Audience
**Target customers (B2C, Bangladesh first):**
1. **Bachelors, students, mess-dwellers, first-job holders** — living away from home, cooking for the first time on a small budget.
2. **Health-conscious households & carers** — someone in the family has type 2 diabetes, needs less salt, is pregnant, or is elderly. The *buyer* is often a son/daughter or spouse, not the patient.
3. **AI-curious learners** — students, job seekers, freelancers, small-business owners who want to use ChatGPT/AI and automation in Bangla, without coding.
**Decision-makers:** the reader themself (bachelor, learner) or a family carer (health books). Mobile-first; Facebook is where they discover and ask.
**Primary use case:** "Tell me exactly what to cook/do, in my language, with ingredients/tools I already have."
**Jobs to be done:**
- Cook decent, cheap home food without calling Ammu every day.
- Plan everyday meals for a family member's condition with familiar Bangladeshi food.
- Learn to use AI for real work/income, in Bangla, step by step.
**Use cases:** first month in a mess; a parent just diagnosed with diabetes; a pregnancy in the family; a student wanting a freelancing edge with AI; an office worker packing lunch.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Useful practical knowledge is mostly in English, scattered across videos, or locked in printed books that are hard to carry and search.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- YouTube/Facebook recipes: no plan, no portions, endless scrolling, ads.
- Printed cookbooks (Rokomari ৳100–450): heavy, not health-specific, not on the phone in the kitchen.
- English AI courses/books: language barrier, expensive (৳1,200–3,750 imports), not BD-context.
- Pirated PDFs on Facebook/Telegram: low quality, outdated, no support.
**What it costs them:** money on outside food/doctor-unfriendly meals; time; missed AI-driven opportunities.
**Emotional tension:** "I should be able to do this"; worry about a parent's health; fear of falling behind on AI.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Bangla PDF ebook sellers on Facebook pages/Telegram (AI ebooks ৳49–299; e.g. Banglatuts ৳299, Uzzala ৳200) — falls short because quality and support vary, payment trust is low, no reader/library.
**Secondary:** Rokomari/Boighor ebooks & printed cookbooks — falls short because general, not health- or bachelor-specific, not structured as action plans.
**Indirect:** Free YouTube/Facebook recipe and AI videos; asking ChatGPT directly — falls short because no structure, no plan, no portions, overwhelming.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Written for specific situations (bachelor, diabetes, low salt, pregnancy, elderly, office lunch) — not generic.
- Every recipe: Bangladeshi ingredients, clear steps, portions; health books add approximate carbs/sodium and 7-day plans.
- AI series is a structured 4-book path with projects, prompts, templates and 30-day roadmaps — in Bangla.
- Own it forever; read on phone in the browser (flip-book reader) or download; personalised copy.
- Look inside before buying; prices below a printed book.
**Why customers choose us:** "It's in Bangla, made for exactly my situation, cheaper than a printed book, and on my phone."

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Is bKash Send Money safe? Will I get the book?" | Order page shows exact amount + reference; book unlocks when the TrxID is checked (usually within [hours]); support@qabasic.com; refund policy for non-delivery. Add WhatsApp/Messenger for questions. |
| "I can find recipes/AI tips free on YouTube." | Free = scattered. This is a plan: 101 recipes with portions + a 7-day plan / a 30-day AI roadmap, offline on your phone. |
| "Is a PDF worth ৳199?" | Less than one restaurant meal / one month of a course; Look inside first. |
| "Is this medical advice?" | No — general guidance with familiar food; follow your doctor's plan. (Never claim health outcomes.) |
**Anti-persona:** people wanting a doctor-prescribed diet plan; people who only want printed books; advanced developers (AI books start from no-code).

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** tired of outside food / confusing diet advice / English-only AI content.
**Pull:** situation-specific, Bangla, cheap, on the phone, looks good.
**Habit:** scrolling free videos; Ammu's phone call; asking ChatGPT ad hoc.
**Anxiety:** paying an unknown site by bKash; PDF quality; "will I actually use it?"
